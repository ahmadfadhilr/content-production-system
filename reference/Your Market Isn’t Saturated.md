Mozi Minute: Your Market Isn’t Saturated

 

Most entrepreneurs think their market is too saturated.

 

It’s one of the main excuses entrepreneurs give for why their business isn’t growing.

 

99% of people who say this are wrong.

 

Your market is probably 100x bigger than you think.

 

The average entrepreneur has tapped into 1% of their market. 

 

Real Story: 

 

When I ran Gym Launch I interviewed a sales rep who wanted to join my team.

 

His previous company sold gym software.

 

I asked him how they generated leads.

 

He told me they did $10 million per month entirely through outbound sales.

 

I had never heard of this company.

 

At the time they were doing five times my revenue using a channel I thought wouldn't work at scale.

This broke the belief for me. 

 

Whenever I hear someone say their market is too small I do two things:


Reframe the problem


Market saturation is an easy excuse because it’s outside of your control.

Meaning there’s nothing that can be done about it.

So we have to start by reframing the problem to something we can control.

Once it’s in your control, you can find a solution.


Reframes I use:


“My market is too small” = “I don't know how to advertise to people who are not already aware of my solution."


"leads cost too much," = "I don't have the skill to make my offer compelling enough.”


“There are no good technicians in my area” = “I don’t know how to recruit, hire and manage good technicians in this area”



Find the solution using: More. Better. New.


More: Most businesses simply aren’t doing enough. 
Ask how you can do more of what’s already working?

"Instead of posting 3 pieces of content a week how can you post 1 per day?"


Better: How can you get better at what’s already working?

If organic content works, analyze the top 10%.

What’s different?

How can you recreate it?

New: Lastly, I’ll try something new.

Doing new is the highest risk of the three so I only do it when I’ve exhausted the other two.

New looks like putting what you do in more places.

Or trying new strategies all together.

If you’re only on instagram...

Join facebook, tik tok, local groups, etc.
 

Hypothetical Example:


You run a local roofing company that relies on door knocking.

And instead of blaming the market size when business stalls you do this:

 

More: You go from 10 reps to 20 reps and increase minimum quota per rep from 100 knocks to 150 knocks per day. This alone is a 3x in inputs.

 

Better: You go from training sales reps once per week to once per day. Every rep is now more efficient.

 

New: You start running Google local service ads and have your top guys close inbound deals.

 

Next time you feel your market is saturated, reframe it and look for a solution.

Because it’s probably not saturated, you just aren't doing enough stuff good enough :)
