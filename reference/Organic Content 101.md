I talked to a room full of business owners for eight hours about organic marketing.

 

Here are the biggest takeaways on creating content and how to build a brand.

 

Your Content Should Only Give. Your Ads Should Ask

 

Most people try to balance giving value and promoting, in the same piece of content.

 

Don't.

 

All of your content should be pure value. 

 

No CTAs. No pitching. Just teach. 

 

Then run ads separately to do the asking.

 

Think about their newsfeed: content from you, content from you, content from you, ad from you. 

 

You separate the give and the ask completely. 

 

It protects your brand and people keep watching.

 

Quality Beats Quantity (Eventually)

 

In the beginning, volume is how you learn quality. 

 

Make a lot of stuff to figure out what works.

 

But over time, spending 10x more time on one video doesn't get 10x the views. 

 

It can get 100x. 

 

Platforms reward quality disproportionately.

 

That's why the biggest creators post less frequently than you'd think.

 

Where Topics Come From

 

Use these four buckets for creating content:

Real Time. I tweet ideas as they come. Those tweets become the source for videos, reels, everything.
Recent Past. Look at your calendar. Every meeting, client problem, or decision is a piece of content.
Far past. Big lessons that shifted how you think over years.
Manufactured. Experiences you create on purpose, like starting a Skool community from scratch and documenting it.
Between those four, we never run dry on content ideas.
